# Burbujas coral

Method: built-in image_gen, new generation of a material texture. The user’s photograph guides the salmon-coral colour and translucent fruit-skin surface. The spheres retain the positions and radii of the earlier three clusters and are rendered as actual 3D spheres.

Asset: `bubble-skin-v1.png`.

## Full prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: seamless surface material texture for small translucent coral-pink spheres in a real-time 3D artwork.
Primary request: a flat macro material scan of translucent pinkcurrant skin, matching a warm pale salmon-coral and rose-peach hue. Very delicate natural wax bloom, tiny pores, faint irregular inner pulp fibres and minute amber inclusions visible through moist thin skin. Slightly milky transparency, subtle tonal variation, a luminous pale coral pink throughout, no strong red or magenta.
Composition: square, flat continuous surface filling the whole frame, even scale, tileable edges. No berries, spheres, leaves, stalks or objects pictured within the texture. Fine realistic photographic microstructure, not a vein diagram or honeycomb. Gentle cloudy pink tissue, not wavy bands or worm shapes.
Lighting: evenly diffuse neutral illumination, no cast shadows, no baked specular highlights, no depth-of-field blur, no background outside the material.
Constraints: no typography, symbols, borders or watermark. This is a texture map, not a finished rendering.

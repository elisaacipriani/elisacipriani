`elisa-drawing-white-layers-v1.png` is an unchanged copy of that photograph. It is projected onto the existing closed 3D drawing in local coordinates, with the photograph's aspect ratio preserved and mirrored sampling at its edges. The runtime builds mipmaps and uses anisotropic filtering for distant views. Its luminance supplies shallow surface relief; continuous fine detail supplements extreme close-ups.

The material is opaque, matte and ivory white on both backgrounds. The drawing geometry, attachment, picking and poem are unchanged.

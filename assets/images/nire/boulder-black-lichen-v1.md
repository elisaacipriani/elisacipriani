## Full prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: transparent RGBA surface overlay texture for a real-time 3D stone. This is a material patch, not a finished scene.
Primary request: natural black dendritic crustose lichen like the user's photographed rock. A broad irregular asymmetric colony of tiny charcoal-black, densely encrusted nodules, branching outward into many uneven short finger-like and fern-like lobes. The lower part is a broken dense field of minute rough black islands and closely packed clusters. Toward the upper half, several distinct meandering larger branching fronds extend into open space, with thin irregular forks and blunt rough tips. Preserve abundant transparent negative space between every branch and cluster; black colonies occupy about 30 percent of the square.
Material: photographic, very fine brittle granular crust, delicate crazing, tiny lifted edges, carbon black and dark graphite with restrained grey microtexture. Very shallow actual-looking mineral encrustation. Uneven growth at many scales, never a repeated pattern. Branches are not regular botanical leaves, roots, trees or drawn vector strokes.
Composition: square flat top-down macro material scan, the patch fills the square with multiple connected and detached colonies; no rock silhouette, no pebble, no landscape, no surrounding surface, no objects. Only black lichen on a genuinely transparent background. Fine detail and natural irregular edges everywhere, no out-of-focus blur.
Lighting: even diffuse neutral lighting, no cast shadow, no baked directional highlights, no background colour. The real 3D renderer supplies light and tiny surface relief.
Constraints: the background and spaces inside the colony are transparent alpha, not white or grey. No text, no labels, no symbols, no watermark, no decorative border. No pale lichen, no green moss, no orange growth.
